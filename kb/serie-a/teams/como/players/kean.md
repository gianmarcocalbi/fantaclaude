# Kean — contested

Como signed him late in the window on a loan they are obliged to convert, and
every report on the move says the same thing: the shirt was not promised. He
shares the centre-forward's place with Douvikas, who reads the system better
and has the slight edge, while Kean brings the power and the runs in behind;
the duel is described as tight, the alternation as frequent, and the two are
named as playable together in some games. He also has last season's form and
condition to recover. Contested is the honest reading of a shared shirt, and
it sits above the rate his own history gives him — that history is not of a
man in this situation. He is named behind Da Cunha as a penalty alternative,
which changes nothing while Da Cunha plays. Paired with [[douvikas]]: if
either note is wrong they are both wrong, in opposite directions.
