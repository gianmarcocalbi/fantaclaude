# Fantacristo

New to the league this season. The author's brother, which is why this dossier
is first-hand rather than impressionistic.

## How he bids

Rather rational in general — he is not a manager who throws credits away — with
one systematic exception: he buys **a lot** of Inter players. Note the shape of
that bias. It is not the Gene pattern of overpaying wildly for one Inter name;
it is volume, a squad that ends up disproportionately Inter across many lots.

That distinction matters at the auction. A volume buyer is beatable on any
single lot, because losing one does not break his plan — he simply takes the
next Inter player. So contesting him on a specific Inter target is usually
cheap, provided we actually want that player.

New to this league, so nothing to record here.

## Watch

- Whether the Inter volume habit survives contact with a room containing three
  other Inter supporters. It may not: there will not be enough Inter players to
  go round, and that scarcity is what will price them.
